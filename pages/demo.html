Interactive Demo — Riemannian Crowd Simulation

Legacy demonstrator. The paper uses a separate scientific implementation.

Two Agents
Shows 2 agents moving through a shared space and how their paths respond to each other.
Overtaking
Demonstrates 2 agents in an overtaking interaction and how the motion adapts during the pass.
Counter Flow
Visualizes 240 agents in opposing streams and how the crowd adjusts near the center of travel.
Bottleneck
Models 224 agents funneling through a narrow opening and highlights congestion behavior.
Circle Antipodal
Places 100 agents on opposite sides of a circle and shows how they steer toward one another.
FPS Benchmark
Measures performance with a customizable number of agents and shows how the simulation scales.
